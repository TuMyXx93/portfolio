# Accessibility SEO Skill

Focus: WCAG compliance and technical SEO consistency.

Rules:

1. Prefer semantic HTML before ARIA fallbacks.
2. Ensure keyboard accessibility and visible focus indicators.
3. Keep metadata complete at route boundaries.
4. Provide meaningful alt text and labels.
5. Validate heading hierarchy per route and keep one clear page-level H1.
6. Ensure canonical URLs, Open Graph, and robots directives are consistent across indexable routes.
7. Do not trade accessibility for animation or visual polish.
